## California (Direct Booking Only)

- **Tomales Bay (Point Reyes)**: Local outfitters (e.g. Blue Waters Kayaking) book directly on selected dark-moon dates. No standalone GetYourGuide listing. Rates vary by operator and date ($95–$145 typical). https://bioluminescentkayaking.us/california/tomales-bay/
- **San Diego (La Jolla / Torrey Pines)**: Natural red-tide blooms cannot be scheduled. Commercial "glow kayak" offerings use battery-powered LED lights, not bioluminescence. Real blooms are tracked live on the site at no charge. https://bioluminescentkayaking.us/california/san-diego/
